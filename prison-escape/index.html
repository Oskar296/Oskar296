BLACKGATE
MAXIMUM SECURITY · PRISON ESCAPE

You're the only ordinary inmate in a wing full of super-villains. Lights are out. The guards are patrolling. BRUTE, VOLT, WRAITH and the WARDEN are between you and the sky. Stay in the shadows, grab the keycards, and get out.

Tip: guards see in a cone of light. Break their line of sight — or duck into a locker.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Move with WASD or the arrow keys (or the on-screen stick on touch).
  • Sprint with SHIFT — faster, but noisy. Guards can hear you run nearby.
  • Vision cones are the light in front of guards & villains. Stay out of them.
  • Being seen fills the ALARM bar. Fill it and you're recaptured. Break line of sight to calm it.
  • Keycards open the colour-matched doors. Collect them all to unlock the EXIT.
  • Lockers — stand next to one and press E / SPACE to hide. You're invisible inside.
